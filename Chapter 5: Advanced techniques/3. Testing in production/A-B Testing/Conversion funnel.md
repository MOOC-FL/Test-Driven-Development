#### Conversion Funnel
- Similar to a purchase funnel, `"conversion funnel"` is a technical term used in **e-commerce operations** to describe the track a consumer takes through an Internet advertising or search system, navigating an e-commerce website and finally converting to a sale.


#### Main Elements of an Online Sales Funnel

| Element | Description | Corresponding Traditional Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traffic Sources** | Channels that bring users to the funnel (e.g., SEO, PPC, referral traffic, etc.) | Pre-funnel (acquisition) |
| **TOFU (Top of the Funnel)** | Coincides with the traditional **awareness** stage. Prospects become aware of your product or solution. | Awareness |
| **MOFU (Middle of the Funnel)** | Describes prospects in the **consideration** stage. They evaluate options, compare competitors, and seek more information. | Interest / Consideration |
| **BOFU (Bottom of the Funnel)** | Corresponds to later life-cycle stages: **decision, conversion, purchase**. The prospect is ready to take action. | Desire / Action (Decision/Conversion/Purchase) |
| **Re-engagement Paths** | Strategies and techniques meant to **recover lost prospects/leads**. Usually executed through retargeting ads or email marketing. | Post-funnel (retention / recovery) |

- The modern conversion funnel can have many entrance points, meaning people can enter at any stage of their life-cycle, they can leave and enter again. This is why an effective online marketing strategy requires an omnichannel approach that combines various traffic sources, campaigns and re-engagement paths, and makes them work as one to finalize the purchase and even lead to loyal customers or brand advocates.
