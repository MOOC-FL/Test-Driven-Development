#### purchase funnel
- The purchase funnel, or purchasing funnel, is a consumer-focused marketing model that illustrates the theoretical customer journey toward the purchase of a good or service.


#### Stages of the Sales Funnel (AIDA Model)

| Stage | Name | Description | Key Seller Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Awareness** | Prospective customer becomes aware that a seller offers a product/solution meeting their needs. Happens via advertising, word of mouth, prospect research, etc. | After awareness, prospect begins considering how to find an appropriate solution. |
| **2** | **Interest** | Prospect expresses interest and goes through an evaluation process: seeks more information, compares competitors, becomes more educated. | Provide a **compelling argument** for the effectiveness of your product. |
| **3** | **Desire** | Prospect needs all information, answers to lingering questions, fears assuaged, and conviction that the action will lead to satisfaction. | **Demonstrate authority** and prove your offering is the best option. |
| **4** | **Action** | Prospective customer completes the process and becomes an **active customer**. | Convert one-time customers into **repeat customers** by giving them exactly what they want (and more), which can also raise awareness and attract new prospects into the funnel. |
