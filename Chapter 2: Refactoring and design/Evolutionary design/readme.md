#### Evolutionary design
> aka incremental design, aka continuous design
- Designing a whole system up-front without mistakes is impossible.At the start of a project is the time when we know the least about the problem we are solving.That's why our design must evolve as we learn more. We put our learning back into the program by modifying it to look as **if we had known what we were doing all along, and as if it had been easy to do.**
- We should start with an idea of a design, but assume it's wrong.We'll continually look for signs that our current design doesn't fit the problem, and then we'll modify the design to fit it better.
- But we shouldn't build flexible and reusable components up-front. They would increase complexity and anyway might not flex in the right direction.Instead, we'll follow the principles of YAGNI and Simple Design to reduce the friction of changing our design into any direction.
